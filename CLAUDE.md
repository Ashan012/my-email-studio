# Email Studio

The email studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business. 

## Folders

- **brand** - Stores brand assets like logos, colors, fonts, and messaging guidelines.
- **leads** - Stores information about potential leads and contacts.
- **campaigns** - Stores email campaign drafts, plans, and content.
- **notes** - Stores research, ideas, and other notes related to campaigns.

## Rules

- Write in plain English.
- Never send email to real people.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
